# 3D stage: camera, calibration, accuracy

How the stream turns the four tip points (forceps and shadow) into a 3D tip position, where its calibration comes
from, and how accurate it is against the simulator's trajectory log. Issue: [#1](https://github.com/oiidmnk/kap-forceps-tracking/issues/1).

## Model

`services/stream/tool_positions.py` reconstructs with a perspective pinhole camera ~22 mm from the eye centre. Without
the `camera_*` fields in the calibration it falls back to the orthographic model used before #1.

## Calibration (`config/default_calibration.json`)

| field | what |
|---|---|
| `light_rot_up`, `light_rot_clock`, `forceps_rot_up`, `forceps_rot_clock` | trocar positions on the eye (rad) |
| `light_depth_mm`, `light_aim_tilt`, `light_aim_clock` | light tip 1.73 mm past its trocar, and its aim |
| `camera_focal_px`, `camera_pp_px` | camera in video px (1080² exported MP4) |
| `camera_center_mm` | camera centre in the eye frame |
| `eye_radius_mm`, `jaw_length_mm` | 12.05 mm, 1.9 mm |
| `trocar_line_deg` | trocar line in the lens-off view that goes with the trocar angles (3.33°) |
| `eye_center_px`, `eye_radius_px` | fallback eye circle; `make calibration` does not push these |

All measured from the trajectory logs of the three runs of 2026-11-09 at 6.5× / 6 mm
(`scripts/eval_sim_gt.py … --write-calibration`, averaged). They hold for the simulator's own image orientation
(capture with `ROT=0`).

The session adds `eye_roll_deg` (set with *Mark trocars*): roll = trocar line − `trocar_line_deg`, added to both
trocar clocks when the calibration goes to the stream. The light aim is relative to its trocar and turns along.

**Eye circle and camera.** The stream wants the camera relative to its eye circle, in the coordinates the points
arrive in (the detector's normalised square). The orchestrator converts the stored px camera with the detector's
current circle (`GET /config`) on every push, so the circle cancels out of the 3D. Re-detecting the circle per
frame would only add noise: the auto-detected centre scatters by 19–33 px between frames while the eye moves by 5–12 px.

## Accuracy vs. the simulator (`make sim-eval`)

The simulator's trajectory log (`<video>_trajectories.csv`: instrument poses in mm) is the ground truth.
`scripts/eval_sim_gt.py` fits camera and log offset from the forceps tips, replays the video through eye circle →
detector → calibration → `tool_positions.py` and compares the tip midpoint (median / p95 in mm).

**Perspective, 6.5× / 6 mm** – three runs of `mapskap1_2026-11-09` (forceps training lev 2, ~3100–3300 frames with the
tip in view each). Camera, trocars and light averaged from the *other two* runs (what a camera measured once gives).
"Limit of the 3D model" uses the ground-truth tip and a modelled shadow.

| run | | orthographic (before #1) | perspective | height | 3D found |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11-14-13 | limit of the 3D model | 1.86 / 6.1 | 0.14 / 0.35 | 0.05 / 0.16 | 100 % |
| | learned segmenter | 1.78 / 8.6 | 0.24 / 6.1 | 0.12 / 2.4 | 84 % |
| 11-25-32 | limit of the 3D model | 1.96 / 6.1 | 0.16 / 0.33 | 0.08 / 0.19 | 100 % |
| | learned segmenter | 1.46 / 6.4 | 0.16 / 0.60 | 0.09 / 0.48 | 65 % |
| 11-32-14 | limit of the 3D model | 2.16 / 10.9 | 0.14 / 0.55 | 0.06 / 0.33 | 100 % |
| | learned segmenter | 1.81 / 9.5 | 0.12 / 0.61 | 0.06 / 0.44 | 69 % |

* Height bias: −0.05 to +0.09 mm (orthographic: −0.38 to −0.53 mm).
* With each run's own camera the segmenter reaches 0.12–0.17 mm, so a camera measured once loses little.
* Stored relative to the auto-detected eye circle instead of in video px, the same camera gave 0.31 / 0.23 / 0.19 mm.
* The p95 of 6 mm in 11-14-13 comes from detection outliers, not from the model.
* At 6.5× / 6 mm the focal length differed by 0.45 % and the camera distance by 0.1 mm between the three runs.

**Orthographic, 2026-10-22** (before #1; recorded before the 6.5× / 6 mm rule): 1.4–2.4 mm median with the segmenter, height
0.5–0.9 mm; the classical detector without background 1.8 mm, 3D found in 49 % of the frames.

## Open points

1. **Eye roll** – between 2026-10-22 and 2026-11-09 the eye sat 4.5–6.6° rolled; 5° costs ~0.1–0.15 mm. Within one day
   the correction does not help: the instruments roll the eye by 1–2° during a run, more than it differs between runs
   (0.3–0.9°). Not yet checked across days with a lens-off view.
2. **TODO: live input = exported video?** The camera is measured on the exported MP4s (1080 × 1080). It holds for the
   live input only with the same framing (same size, no extra crop or scaling). Expected, not yet checked: compare a
   live frame with an exported one, e.g. the trocars in the lens-off view.
3. **Reporting rate** – on the lev2 runs the stream reports the tip in only 65–84 % of the frames (shadow detection).

## Commands

```bash
make sim-eval SIM=<video>                       # trajectory CSV next to it; DET=classic for the classical detector
.venv-ml/bin/python scripts/eval_sim_gt.py <video> --reuse --calibration <json>        # score another calibration on cached detections
.venv-ml/bin/python scripts/eval_sim_gt.py <video> --reuse --write-calibration <json>  # calibration the recording implies
```

Results in `artifacts/sim_gt_*`. To record a run for evaluation: export video + trajectory log, and show the lens-off
view for a few seconds (trocars).
